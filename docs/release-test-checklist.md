# In-game release checks

Use a test client with Slayer Task Odds enabled. Record the character, master, selected task, blocked tasks and screenshots for any mismatch. Odds are estimates when a master's block list is marked unconfirmed or old.

1. **Startup and themes:** Log in and open the panel. Select Legacy and Scroll with each colour, then Fractured Archive. Check the title, task selector, portraits, result rows, expanded sections and footer for clipping at your usual client size. Confirm changing Theme Colours does not alter Fractured Archive. Switch themes with sections open and confirm their state and selected task are retained.
2. **Live odds:** Select a task that several masters assign. At a master, open Slayer rewards → Tasks and compare several percentages with that master's panel values, allowing for the game's one-decimal rounding. Check a task the master cannot assign and hover its zero-chance explanation.
3. **Block sync:** Open the Tasks screen for a master, sync its list and verify each slot and the sync status. Block or unblock a task in game, reopen the screen and check that its odds and the other task odds update. Repeat with a different master and verify the lists remain separate.
4. **Manual editing:** Close the task screen, edit one block in Saved block lists and confirm the master's odds change. While that master's task screen is open, attempt a manual edit; confirm it is rejected with a concise chat message. Restore your actual blocks and resync.
5. **Character isolation:** Log into another character on the same RuneLite profile and verify the previous character's blocks and selected task are not silently carried across. Return to the first character and check its saved data. If older blocks are imported, check that only unconfirmed masters show an approximate marker.
6. **Master access and filters:** Disable and re-enable individual masters, including Mortimer. Verify the comparison and routing candidates update, and that changing several switches does not spam chat. Toggle hiding 0.00% masters and expand them to read their explanations.
7. **Mortimer:** Visit him, open his tasks and sync his separate blocks. Check his completion count, access requirements and the number of offered tasks. Compare the panel's task appearance chance and hover breakdown with the in-game offer behaviour. Expand modifiers and check their unlock states and estimate labels; these are estimates, not confirmed exact probabilities.
8. **Routing:** With Shortest Path installed, enable routing and select a task. Confirm the destination is the highest-odds eligible regular master. Check that Mortimer is only eligible for routing when his separate routing setting is enabled and his location has been observed. Disable routing afterward if you do not want it left on.
9. **Previous task and cape:** Finish a task and compare its chance with the in-game task list. Where available, check the previous-task explanation with a Slayer cape in inventory or equipped, and without one.
10. **Release notes and persistence:** Open What's new and the full changelist, check scrolling and readability, then restart the client and verify your chosen theme, blocks and sync state persist. Toggle sync chat messages off and confirm routine sync messages and reminders are suppressed.
